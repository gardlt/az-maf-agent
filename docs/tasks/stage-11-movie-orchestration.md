## Five-theater operations, seasonality and price-simulation tasks

- [ ] **S11-12 — Build the authoritative synthetic theater backend.** Depends on S11-01/03/04. Load [simulation defaults](../stages/stage-11/simulation-defaults.json): two small venues (2 × 80 seats each), two medium (4 × 150), one large (8 × 250), with 20 screens/3,520 seats overall. Define screens/seats, sellable capacity, films/showings, turnaround/maintenance, holds, ticket reservations, event appointments, published prices and versions. Use actual persistent test state and reviewed transaction semantics; expose a separate scoped operations contract without making TMDB writable. Enforce owner/staff/customer roles and per-location authorization independently of prompts. Deliver `operations-backend-results.json`.
- [ ] **S11-13 — Implement ticket and private/group reservations.** Depends on S11-12 and Stage 8/9 action/data contracts. Support availability lookup, bounded holds, confirmation, cancellation and private/group-event appointments; prevent seat, screen and time-interval conflicts atomically. Use exact idempotency/version/expiry checks, locked price/currency quotes and verified commit receipts that say simulated. Test concurrent last-seat/last-slot allocation, hold expiry, duplicate confirm/cancel, customer substitution, private-event/public-show overlap and ambiguous commits. No payment gateway, PAN/CVV, real customer data or refund processing. Deliver `booking-results.json`.
- [ ] **S11-14 — Add venue/movie performance analytics.** Depends on S11-12 and frozen synthetic operating-data ground truth. Compute occupancy, attendance/reservations/no-shows, revenue, contribution and trends deterministically by movie/showing/venue/time. Define denominators, period, cost completeness, tax/currency and missing/refunded/cancelled-data treatment. Never call gross revenue net profit or expose raw customer rows to aggregate-only agents. Distinguish poor slot/price/venue performance from film-wide failure. Deliver `business-metrics-results.json`.
- [ ] **S11-15 — Research and evaluate seasonal programming.** Depends on S11-04/14. Join approved movie facts with region/time-zone/year calendar features and synthetic/historical venue patterns. Treat Halloween/horror and Christmas/holiday associations as hypotheses; test local audience differences, release age, promotions, sparse history and cold-start uncertainty. TMDB attention scores are not local sales/profit data. Backtest with time-based holdouts, no future-data leakage and no-seasonality/current-program baselines. Propose per-location films/showings with rights/availability uncertainty and required owner review. Deliver `seasonality-results.json`.
- [ ] **S11-16 — Calculate constrained simulated ticket prices.** Depends on S11-12/14/15 and a reviewed objective. Implement the configurable $12 reference/$8–$18 half-dollar grid and explicitly synthetic demand/cost assumptions in the defaults. Maximize modeled contribution subject to capacity, currency, approved bounds, locked quotes and no personal/protected-attribute personalization. Use deterministic currency arithmetic and versioned inputs, report uncertainty/sensitivity and current-price/no-seasonality comparisons; do not claim a universally best price or guaranteed profit. Owner approval is required before price publication; no existing hold/booking may be repriced. Private/group-event quotes need a distinct reviewed policy. Deliver `pricing-simulation-results.json` with ordinary/Halloween/Christmas/sparse-data cases and reproducible example calculations.
- [ ] **S11-17 — Validate business writes, forecasts and failure modes.** Depends on S11-12–16 and S11-06/07/09. Test five-venue data/write isolation, forged approval/price/seasonal inputs, insufficient film facts/rights, overlapping schedules, double bookings, demand/cost corruption, unsupported forecasts, cancellation during commit, stale recommendations and rollback. Verify approved publish/book/appointment effects against actual synthetic state; denied writes have zero unauthorized effects. Preserve shared root budgets and deterministic aggregate/price outputs through retries. Deliver `business-simulation-security-results.json` and feed the Stage 8 acceptance harness before S11-10.

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] Orchestrator and specialists perform real bounded inter-agent work with distinct authenticated identity/service boundaries in the accepted hosted scope; the selected protocol is accurately described.
- [ ] Each agent has only its approved role/data/tools; one runtime cannot impersonate peers or alter controller/operator policy. Shared blueprint/identity resources are not disabled to contain one agent.
- [ ] Root and child work share existing limits, expiry, owner scope, audit and quarantine; duplicate/retried/resumed/parallel tasks cannot refill authority or accounting.
- [ ] Proposals use verified movie facts and validated source references, satisfy the selected five-venue constraints and visibly identify synthetic/unknown data. Simulated reservations/showtimes reflect committed backend state; no live booking, payment, rights or guaranteed-profit claim is fabricated.
- [ ] Forged/hostile peer content, cross-user access, arbitrary endpoints, privilege escalation and exfiltration yield zero unauthorized effects in the frozen suite.
- [ ] Parent/child/individual-agent failure and recovery are measured, preserve unrelated agents and reservations, fence stale output and never auto-switch identity to bypass disable.
- [ ] Selected capacity totals, both reservation types, pricing/seasonality calculations and per-venue business metrics pass deterministic and adversarial checks; simulated writes are properly scoped, approved where required and never double book.
- [ ] Hosted evidence, privacy-safe correlation, comparative value and operational rollback are reviewed against exact versions; prior Stage 5/7/8/9/10 gates remain applicable.
