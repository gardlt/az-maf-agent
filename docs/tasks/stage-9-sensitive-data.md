# Stage 9 tasks — PII, payment-data and receipt guardrails

Status: **planned; no implementation or acceptance complete**, 2026-10-20.

[Stage guide and sources](../stages/stage-9/README.md) · [QA/evidence plan](../stages/stage-9/validation/README.md) · [Roadmap](README.md)

## Ordered tasks and deliverables

- [ ] **S9-01 — Select the sensitive-data use case and scope.** Identify whether the app merely rejects sensitive inputs or supports an approved receipt/document workflow. Inventory field types, sources, purposes, jurisdictions, actors, all processors and destinations. Assign real privacy/security/payment owners; verify PCI applicability/standard and relevant obligations without declaring certification. Begin with synthetic data and keep real payment processing out of scope. Deliver `data-scope.md`.
- [ ] **S9-02 — Freeze a field-level handling policy.** Depends on S9-01 and Stage 8 S8-16/S8-18 interfaces. Specify allow/redact/tokenize/reject rules, authorized roles/purposes, raw versus derived representations, prohibited authentication data and permitted downstream fields. Review the proposed defaults before code. Distinguish mask/truncate/reversible tokenization and prohibit general agent/model access to full PAN or CVV/PIN/track data. Deliver `sensitive-data-policy.json` and policy review references.
- [ ] **S9-03 — Build bounded intake and extraction controls.** Depends on S9-02 and a selected receipt input format. Authenticate ownership; validate MIME/signature, size/page/resource bounds and file names; isolate parsing/OCR with no arbitrary code, URLs or attachment fetching. Inspect text/images, hidden layers, annotations, metadata and QR/barcode contents. Fail/review unsupported, encrypted or unreadable files. Treat extracted instructions as untrusted. Deliver `intake-results.json`; no upload route is enabled just by this task.
- [ ] **S9-04 — Implement evaluated detection and redaction.** Depends on S9-02/03 and approved service/data-destination decisions. Compare deterministic validators and any candidate PII/OCR services on frozen synthetic multilingual/format fixtures. Define per-category precision/recall, false-negative/false-positive and extraction thresholds before scoring. Redact before model/tool persistence or dispatch; preserve offsets/bounding-box correctness without retaining prohibited originals. Validate redacted artifacts by re-extraction/rendering, not visual masking alone. Deliver `redaction-evaluation.json` and `detector-decision.md`.
- [ ] **S9-05 — Enforce model/tool/output boundaries.** Depends on S9-02/04, S9-12 and Stage 8 streaming/DLP/output/authority interfaces. Inspect text, image/file references, tool arguments/results, generated answers, chunk boundaries, citations/links and exports. Allow only approved derived fields; prevent encoded/reconstructed values, cross-user receipt access, log/error leaks and prompt-injection bypass. A classifier's benign result cannot grant authority. Deliver `boundary-results.json`.
- [ ] **S9-06 — Design scoped storage and token references.** Depends on S9-01/02 and explicit decisions on whether storage or reversible tokenization is necessary. Keep raw protected data/vault authority outside the model-facing runtime; prefer minimized structured records and opaque owner/purpose-bound references. Define encryption/key ownership, scoped identity/RBAC, access review, re-identification controls and no credentials in model context. Never rely on encryption to permit prohibited authentication-data storage. Deliver `storage-design.md` and negative-permission results; no backend is selected yet.
- [ ] **S9-07 — Govern receipt extraction and integrity.** Depends on S9-03–06. Validate allowed merchant/date/currency/total/tax/item fields, mark uncertainty and missing values, verify arithmetic without inventing values, and detect duplicate/tampered receipts with scoped references. Separate a merchant receipt, a provider transaction confirmation and the existing simulated-action receipt; none is interchangeable authorization. Refund/payment/reimbursement remains outside scope unless separately approved through Stage 8 consequential-action controls. Deliver `receipt-results.json`.
- [ ] **S9-08 — Enforce retention, deletion and subject access.** Depends on S9-01/06 and explicit retention/hold decisions. Track input/derived documents, temporary files, parser/OCR/detector jobs, session history, checkpoints, caches, exports, backups/versions and provider copies. Implement owner-authorized inspection/correction/deletion where applicable, clear deadlines and downstream cleanup verification; distinguish retrieval exclusion from physical deletion and remaining backup/provider obligations. Do not change the existing movie preference policy. Deliver `lifecycle-results.json`.
- [ ] **S9-09 — Add private audit and incident response.** Depends on S9-02/05 and Stage 7/8 incident controls. Record actor, scoped record reference, policy/detector version, data category, decision and counts without sensitive values. Prevent raw payloads in traces, exceptions, alert notifications and evidence. Test detector/parser outages, failure escalation, scoped quarantine, privileged review access and authorized recovery without exposing unrelated users. Deliver `incident-audit-results.json`.
- [ ] **S9-10 — Run adversarial sensitive-data QA.** Depends on selected S9-03–09 controls. Freeze synthetic ground truth and evaluate text/images/OCR errors, supported languages, obfuscation/encoding, split chunks, secret/PAN-like data, mixed receipts, hidden layers, malicious instructions, cross-user access, detector outages, deletion races and denied re-identification. Inspect before/after storage/context/egress effects, not model refusals. Preserve failed/blocked cases; no real customer/cardholder data in fixtures or reports. Deliver `adversarial-results.json` and artifact hashes.
- [ ] **S9-11 — Integrate release gates and review.** Depends on S9-01–10, S9-12–15 and applicable Stage 5/7/8 acceptance. Add local/authorized-hosted checks to the Stage 8 harness, source/policy/version manifests and drift/re-evaluation triggers. Update architecture/data-flow/runbook docs with actual environments, retention, access and limits. Require accountable privacy/security/payment review before real sensitive data; keep unresolved PCI scope, false-negative or untracked-copy issues open. Deliver `sensitive-data-review.md` without claiming certification.

- [ ] **S9-12 — Map and verify guardrail enforcement layers.** Depends on S9-01/02; perform before selecting detectors or configuring model-boundary controls. Inventory actual Foundry model policies on GPT-5 mini/GPT-5, API versions, regions, risk/category/intervention support, blocking/redaction/annotation behavior and trusted request-time policy selection. Record that our Container Apps agent does not automatically receive Foundry Agent Service guardrails. If Agent Service is introduced later, test custom-agent override versus model inheritance and supported tool moderation explicitly; never assume policy stacking or MCP support. Specify the independent application/resource gates for every uncovered path, including receipt input before model dispatch, tool responses before context/storage and streamed output before release. Deny unsupported routes or failed detectors instead of silently bypassing controls. Test both routes, direct model calls, non-model tool/storage paths, weaker/missing policy assignment, untrusted override attempts and configuration drift. Deliver `guardrail-layer-matrix.md` and `guardrail-layer-results.json`; these are prerequisites for S9-05 and final review.

## Additional user-control and disclosure tasks

These specialize existing Stage 4 memory lifecycle and Stage 8 authority/output safeguards for sensitive data; they do not create duplicate implementations or enable new data collection.

- [ ] **S9-13 — User-visible control of sensitive memories and derived records.** Depends on S9-02/06/08 and existing Stage 4 owner-scoped memory controls. If sensitive derived records are approved, let an authenticated owner view the allowed stored fields and provenance/purpose/expiry, correct them, delete an individual record and forget all eligible records. Explain what is not stored and distinguish immediate recall exclusion from physical deletion and any approved hold/backup/provider limits. Do not display raw PAN, prohibited authentication data or privileged token-vault values in a memory viewer. Propagate correction/deletion fences through active runs, checkpoints, caches and summaries; explicit consent does not override prohibited collection. Test foreign-owner access, guessed identifiers, export/inspection scope, concurrent correction/delete/recall, stale summary resurrection and denied re-identification. Deliver `user-data-controls-results.json`; reuse existing preference commands where their contract fits rather than silently widening them.
- [ ] **S9-14 — Purpose-bound action scopes across users and tools.** Specializes Stage 8 S8-18; depends on S9-02/05 and the actual tool/resource inventory. Verify authorization for each sensitive operation against tenant, user, agent, tool, operation, resource and approved purpose. Prevent approval, consent, cached token/reference or delegated permission from one context being reused in another. Approval for extraction is not permission to email/export, reimburse, update a record or re-identify a token. Keep ambient broad credentials away from model-selected destinations and recheck current authority on resume/retry. Test cross-user/tool substitution, forged consent, stale approval, changed arguments/purpose and confused-deputy paths with positive owner controls. Deliver `sensitive-action-scope-results.json`.
- [ ] **S9-15 — Sensitive-output rendering and link/image exfiltration protection.** Specializes Stage 8 S8-17; depends on S9-05 and the selected CLI/web/export renderers. Validate and sanitize generated Markdown/HTML/structured fields before publication, including incremental streaming. Prohibit active content, unsafe URL schemes, unapproved remote images and automatically fetched URLs/attachments. Ensure generated citation links, query strings, redirects, image URLs, previews, downloads and exports cannot carry protected receipt/identity fields to an unapproved destination. Use synthetic secret canaries and a controlled capture endpoint to observe network effects without real sensitive data. Test encoded/fragmented links, streamed markup across chunk boundaries, deceptive labels, raw HTML and copy/export paths; preserve authorized source citations. Deliver `sensitive-rendering-results.json` and documented renderer/egress limitations.

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] Purpose, categories, recipients, accountable owners, retention and PCI applicability are reviewed for the actual selected scope before real data is processed.
- [ ] Prohibited authentication data and unapproved raw PII/card data never reach general model context, tools, memory, logs or evidence in the frozen tests; uncertain/unsupported processing fails closed or enters a separately authorized secure review path.
- [ ] Per-category detection quality meets predeclared thresholds across approved formats/languages; limitations and false negatives remain visible. No perfect-detection guarantee is made.
- [ ] Redacted documents cannot reveal covered values through text extraction, hidden layers, metadata, images, encoded contents or exports in the selected test scope.
- [ ] Receipt ownership, derived-field integrity, per-action permission, vault/re-identification boundaries and cross-user/agent denials pass with positive controls.
- [ ] Temporary/derived/provider copies and deletion/retention obligations are inventoried and verified to the approved policy; encryption or UI masking is not treated as erasure.
- [ ] Outage, abuse, injection and exfiltration cases preserve audit/accounting and prevent sensitive dispatch; privacy-safe evidence and human recovery are proven.
- [ ] Both model routes have verified effective policies; agent inheritance/override and tool applicability are tested if used. Container Apps app/resource gates cover unsupported paths, and no untrusted request can weaken policy.
- [ ] Owners can inspect/correct/delete approved sensitive records within the reviewed lifecycle policy; other users/tools cannot reuse their consent, references or action permissions.
- [ ] Selected renderers and exports prevent tested link/image/markup exfiltration paths, including streamed fragments, while preserving permitted citations.
- [ ] Final review states the exact application scope, test coverage, remaining risks and approval authority. PCI compliance/certification and universal anonymization are not inferred from local tests.
